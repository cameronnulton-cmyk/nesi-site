What we do

Chassis and equipment manufacturers each have their own interface. We translate between them so your build goes together cleanly and works the way the operator expects.

Variable engine speed control

Set and hold engine speed from the equipment controls, within the chassis manufacturer's limits.

Remote start / stop

Start and stop the engine from the crane or remote, with the interlocks the chassis requires.

Engine data readout

Bring engine information from the vehicle network to the operator's display.

In-cab indicators & warnings

Custom lights and alerts for equipment status, so the driver always knows what the body is doing.

One-off harnesses

Custom wiring harnesses built for your chassis and equipment combination, or supplied through a harness partner.

Documentation

Clear wiring diagrams and install and setup instructions delivered with every integration.

Who it's for

Upfitters, truck body builders and equipment dealers who mount cranes and other bodies on trucks, especially when the crane brand and chassis brand weren't designed to work together, or when a chassis change breaks what you used to install.

What we do

Chassis and equipment manufacturers each have their own interface. We translate between them so your build goes together cleanly and works the way the operator expects.

Engine speed & PTO control

Set and hold engine speed in PTO mode from the equipment controls, within the chassis manufacturer's limits, and monitor PTO state.

Remote start / stop

Start and stop the engine from the crane or remote, with the interlocks the chassis requires.

J1939 data & messaging

Bring engine and vehicle information from the J1939 network to the operator's display, and send the messages your equipment needs.

In-cab indicators & warnings

Custom lights and alerts for equipment status, so the driver always knows what the body is doing.

One-off harnesses

Custom wiring harnesses built for your chassis and equipment combination, or supplied through a harness partner.

Documentation

Clear wiring diagrams and install and setup instructions delivered with every integration.

Who it's for

Upfitters, truck body builders and equipment dealers who mount cranes and other bodies on trucks.

The problem

Chassis makers offer options to make electrical integration easier, on production units and custom modifications alike, but many builders never use them. Instead, wires get run by hand behind the dash for engine start/stop, PTO controls, engine speed, lighting and warning signals, and efficiency is lost on every build.

Generic instructions

The equipment manufacturer shipping you a crane or body doesn't know which truck you'll mount it on, and that shows in the generic wiring instructions you get. We create a clean solution specific to your truck and equipment.

About NESI

NESI (Nulton Equipment Systems Integration) is a Pennsylvania company focused on making equipment and vehicles work together reliably.

Our background is in the boom truck industry, mounting heavy-duty cranes on Class 6-8 trucks. That work means controlling remote engine start/stop, engine speed in PTO mode, PTO state monitoring and J1939 messaging. NESI and its partners bring 10 years of experience in integration and custom solutions in the bodybuilder and upfitting industry. Founder Cameron Nulton leads the company.

We work wherever you are. We start by getting familiar with your facility, the products you build, and how your processes work today. From there we decide together how closely we need to work on each project.
